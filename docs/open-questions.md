# Open questions

Decisions that shape the plan. Roughly in order of how much they change it.

1. **What does it look like?** Realistic, low-poly (simple shapes with flat colour), painterly, or deliberately rough like early PlayStation games, which suits spooky? This decides most of the drawing work. Moodboard pictures would help.
2. **Which stack?** Proposed: **TypeScript and Three.js in the browser**, like the aquarium is heading towards, so it can be published and shared as a link, and code and habits carry across. Alternatives: a game engine such as Godot (better tools for 3D scenes and lighting, harder to share as a link), or TouchDesigner (good for live visuals, less suited to a world built from code).
3. **How spooky?** Uneasy and atmospheric, or actually unsettling: weird villages, a figure at the roadside, a driver who is not quite right? It decides how far the weird villages and the driver go.
4. **Who is driving?** Seen or unseen, someone or no one, if the view ever turns to them.
5. **Is it the same drive every time?** A fixed seed gives the same road for everyone; a new seed each time gives a new drive. Perhaps the seed is in the link, like the aquarium's shared tanks.
6. **How British is "British"?** One kind of countryside, or regions that change: moorland, Cotswold stone walls, Welsh hills, Fenland flats? The place names are already from all over the UK.
