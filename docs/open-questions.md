# Open questions

Decisions that shape the plan. Roughly in order of how much they change it.

1. **Watch or drive?** Does it drive itself, so you just watch, or can you steer, choose turnings at junctions, change speed, switch the lights? Watching is simpler and suits an installation; choosing turnings fits a road that is only generated as far as it needs to be.
2. **What does it look like?** Realistic, low-poly (simple shapes with flat colour), painterly, or deliberately rough like early PlayStation games? This decides most of the drawing work. Moodboard pictures would help.
3. **Which stack?** Proposed: **TypeScript and Three.js in the browser**, like the aquarium is heading towards, so it can be published and shared as a link, and code and habits carry across. Alternatives: a game engine such as Godot (better tools for 3D scenes and lighting, harder to share as a link), or TouchDesigner (good for live visuals, less suited to a world built from code).
4. **Is it the same drive every time?** A fixed seed gives the same road for everyone; a new seed each time gives a new drive. Perhaps the seed is in the link, like aquarium's shared tanks.
5. **How British is "British"?** One kind of countryside (say, rolling southern farmland), or regions that change: moorland, the Cotswolds' stone walls, Welsh hills?
6. **How much is the car's inside seen?** Barely (it is about the road), or a lot (the dashboard, mirror and your hands are part of the mood)?
