# Vision

The working name is **Cat's Eyes**, after the reflective studs that light up down the middle of British roads when headlights catch them.

## In one sentence

An endless drive at night through the British countryside, seen from the driver's seat, where everything ahead is generated as you go and lit only by your headlights.

## Where it comes from

Driving around country lanes late at night: the dark beyond the headlights, the hedges sliding past, a sign warning of a bend you cannot see yet, the odd pair of headlights coming over a rise and then gone again. Calm, a little eerie, mostly solitary.

It is also Eleanor's project for learning **generative 3D**: building a world from rules and a random seed instead of modelling it by hand.

## What it is like

- **You are in the car.** The view is from the driver's seat, with some of the car's inside visible: dashboard, windscreen edges, perhaps mirrors.
- **The road goes on for ever** and never repeats, but it is not random noise: it bends, rises, narrows to a single-track lane, widens to a B-road, passes through villages.
- **The dark matters.** The headlights are the main light. What they reach is detailed; what they do not is black, or barely there under a moon.
- **Signs match the road.** A double-bend sign means a double bend is coming. A village's name sign comes before the village. A 30 limit before the houses.
- **Other cars are rare.** Headlights appear in the distance or over a hill, pass with someone visible inside, and are gone.
- **It is British countryside**: hedgerows, stone walls, field gates, oaks and hawthorns, telegraph poles, the odd farm or pub.

## Who it is for

Someone who wants to sit and watch, as a calm piece to leave running, or something to project large. Whether you can also drive it is an [open question](open-questions.md).

## What it is not

Not a driving game: no score, no crashes, no destination.
