# Vision

The working name is **Cat's Eyes**, after the reflective studs that light up down the middle of British roads when headlights catch them.

## In one sentence

An endless, slightly spooky drive at night along British back roads, seen from the passenger seat, where everything ahead is generated as you go and lit mostly by headlights.

## Where it comes from

Being driven along country lanes late at night: the dark beyond the headlights, hedges and trees sliding past, a sign warning of a bend you cannot see yet, following another car's tail lights for a while until it turns off at a roundabout. Eleanor's [first notes](first-notes.md) are kept as written.

It is also Eleanor's project for learning **generative 3D**: building a world from rules and a random seed instead of modelling it by hand.

## What it is like

- **You are the passenger.** Someone else is driving. You look out through the windscreen, feel every bump, and perhaps can turn to look at the driver.
- **Mostly spooky.** Few cars, a lot of dark, things half-seen at the edge of the headlights. Not horror: the unease of a lonely road at night.
- **The road goes on for ever** and never repeats: back roads and country lanes, bends, rises, roundabouts, roadworks, the occasional village, and now and then a strange one.
- **The light comes from cars.** Your headlights, and sometimes the car in front, whose headlights throw a wedge of light across the trees ahead of it. Oncoming headlights now and then. Beyond them, black, or barely there under a moon or a flash of lightning.
- **Signs make sense but the names do not.** A double-bend sign means a double bend is coming; a roundabout sign shows the roundabout's real exits. But the places they point to are real place names from all over the UK, mixed together, so Penzance is 4 miles and Thurso 6.
- **The roadside is British and specific.** Hedgerows, fields, different kinds of tree, bollards, cat's eyes, fluorescent markers, roadkill, bits of plastic caught in the hedge.
- **The car is physical.** It jitters on the road surface and jolts over bumps.

## Who it is for

Someone who wants to sit and watch, as a piece to leave running or to project large. As a passenger you do not steer; what, if anything, you can do is an [open question](open-questions.md).

## What it is not

Not a driving game: no steering, no score, no crashes, no destination.
